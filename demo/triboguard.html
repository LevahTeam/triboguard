Selectivity the compound didn’t cause

“% cytotoxicity” is measured against an untreated control read the same day. A dividing control has been gaining cells all day; a non-dividing one has not. So a compound that kills no cell and only slows division scores in one arm and not the other.

The study compared against lymphocytes, which barely divide.

One identical treatment in both arms: it stops the share of divisions needed to reproduce the reported 40% at 24 h and adds no death. No growth rate is needed for the core result: any control whose cells divide at least 0.0213 times per hour can produce that 40% with nothing dying. This reproduces the absence of cytotoxicity in the lymphocytes, not their reported rise in viability, which no cell-count model produces and which stays unexplained.

The microscope’s blind spot — pre-registered

Imaging is only better than MTS if the software can see the cells. In each image, half the hand-outlined cells were damaged by coin flip and half left alone, so both groups share the same lighting and focus. The pass/fail rules were fixed before three of the four cell lines were measured.

What a cell count can still conclude

A cell the software cannot see counts exactly like a cell that is gone. So a count drop is not a number but a range — and when the range covers both “nothing happened” and “a real effect”, the honest answer is to abstain.

Gone = [max(0, (1−R−b)/(1−b)), 1−R], where R is the counted ratio and b the blindness. “Gone” means dead or never born; telling those apart is the first problem on this page.

Analyse your own counts

One row per well, one number per timepoint. Cell counts, not absorbance — a metabolic reading cannot count cells.

The mean of a birth–death process depends on birth minus death, so killing and stalling can produce identical average curves. Only the variance between wells depends on birth plus death. Intervals here come from the chi-squared law of a sample variance on wells − 1 degrees of freedom — the same arithmetic as the Python package, with exact quantiles tabulated rather than approximated. Every number on this page is produced by the project’s own scripts; the full account, including every error found and corrected, is in the technical report.
